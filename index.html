    How Holdfast works

    A 2016 Pig table, uploaded in 2023, finished in 2026.

    The name

    Holdfast is the 2026 name. The game is still Pig: roll, risk the round, hold to bank it. Seats start as North and South. Type a name. It is kept on this browser.

    Two scores

    The big number is the bank. The red box is the round, still at risk. The bar is the bank against the race length. Wins under the name are the match, not this race.

    A turn

    The grey side is the seat that may roll. Roll as often as you like, then hold. A bust, a wipe, or a hold passes the turn. The tape under the board records the race.

    Roll, bust, wipe

    Both dice count. A 4 and a 5 add 9. A 1 on either die loses the round only. Both sixes set that bank to 0. A 1 is checked first, so a 1 and a 6 is a bust.

    Hold and the race

    Hold banks the round. Holding on 0 does nothing. You win only on banked points, when the bank reaches the race length. The race length is read when New race is pressed. Blank or under 20 falls back to 100.

    Keys

    Space rolls. H holds. N starts a race. ? hides these notes. Typing in a name or the race box does not fire those keys.
