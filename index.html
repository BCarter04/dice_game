    How Holdfast works

    A 2016 Pig table, uploaded in 2023, finished in 2026.

    The name

    Holdfast is the 2026 name. The game is still Pig: roll, risk the round, hold to bank it. Seats start as North and South. A typed name is kept on this browser.

    Two scores

    The big number is the bank. The red box is the round, still at risk. The bar is the bank against the race length. Wins under the name are the match, not this race. Clear match zeros that count only.

    A turn

    The grey seat may roll. Both dice count. A 1 on either die loses the round only. Both sixes set that bank to 0. Hold banks the round. You win only on banked points.

    Keys

    Space rolls. H holds. N starts a race. ? shows these notes. Typing in a name or the race box does not fire those keys.
